## Objective and information contract

Every distinct Sigma2 signature has unit design weight, including nominal-compatible archives. This is the old census measure, not a probability inferred from world or omission-parent multiplicity. At paid history H, T(H) contains all compatible design signatures and W2(H) contains **every** compatible physical M2 realization. A mixed physical signature cell remains mixed. Definite P0 is supported only when W2 is nonempty and all its members agree with that same P0.

At a no-alarm leaf, support reward is

    w(H) = |T(H) intersect Sigma0| * I[P0 is definite and supported by all W2(H)].

It counts justified **original-signature proposals** only. Withdrawal, unknown, the opposite conclusion, valid evidence IDs, and originally irreducible proposals receive no reward. New-signature claim outcomes remain diagnostics. Separate total support from newly restored support above H0, keeping the initially unsupported denominator and complete-archive ceiling visible.

Joint policy J and the sequential baseline know M0, full hypothetical M2 semantics, costs, budget, and paid history. They receive no realized signature, actual k, physical parent, omissions, realized target truth, or unqueried actual answer. Compared with old E, this adds hypothetical physical claim semantics beyond the observable envelope; it is supplied model knowledge, not newly acquired evidence. J versus E is therefore not an ordering-only comparison under identical old information.

The new frontier retains nondominated triples `(d,w,l)`: detected design signatures, supported-original reward, and summed added cost over **all** design signatures. Higher d/w and lower l are preferred. First nominal conflict still ends acquisition. Canonical J maximizes d, then w among maximum-detection policies, then minimizes l, followed by frozen deterministic ties. No scalar error-cost weighting or post-alarm investigation is added. [METHOD.md](METHOD.md) states the recurrence, state sufficiency, projection, and full-budget proofs.
