# Bounded archive-model misspecification: analysis protocol

This review-informed follow-up asks: **when a finite archive model omits a bounded context-receipt-loss mechanism, which nominal conclusions remain justified after that mechanism is admitted, and can the permitted observations expose the mismatch?** It changes admissible retention histories and the interpretation of a named completeness assumption. It does not change probabilities, measure investigator cognition, or repeat the completed acquisition-policy comparison.

The original acquisition model already permits some missing records. This study measures the consequences of adding particular missing possibilities to its finite support. That support expansion can weaken certainty is a logical fact; the empirical quantities here are which fixed cases change, their exact signature counts, and whether the original acquisition policy observes a contradiction. Previously examined problems make this a post-baseline sensitivity analysis, not independent confirmation or pristine held-out research. Null, adverse, coincident, and unaffected conditions remain results.
