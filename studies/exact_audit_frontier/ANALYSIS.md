This review-informed calculation asks how close the preserved audit orders are to the best deterministic conflict detection attainable after nominal acquisition stops, and how much of a remaining gap requires prospective search. It uses already studied finite inputs. The new numerical outcomes are optimality gaps and tradeoffs; exactness, sound alarms, and full-budget detection are validation properties, not independent empirical confirmation.

## Fixed design population, budgets, and arms

The hypothetical design population at a root is the set T(H0) of distinct k=2 complete observable signatures compatible with H0. Each signature has weight one, including nominal-compatible signatures that cannot yield an alarm. This census objective is equivalent to a uniform measure on these cells; it is not a calibrated deployment prior, a prior on worlds, or a weighting by omission parents. All nominal witnesses must embed in the fixed envelope.

For each root let R be the sum of positive integer costs of its unqueried catalogue actions. Compute the exact frontier and every comparator's design point at every integer extra allowance b=0,…,R. Retain the percentage anchors floor(pR/100), p∈{0,25,50,100}, even when they coincide. At those anchors evaluate all six arms on actual k=0,1,2 by qualifying their matching executed paths with retrospective support. The input-derived [configuration](config.json) fixes 1,722 evaluation root/budget cells and 30,510 design-policy runs, plus 2,880 anchor cells and 30,096 anchor runs; unavailable outputs remain explicit. Development has 15 roots, 133 root/budget cells, 2,520 design runs, and 288 anchor cells with 2,928 runs. These schedules are determined from retained inputs, not selected outcomes. Equal allowance never implies equal actual spending.

| Arm | Fixed rule and information |

Selectors receive declared models, initial evidence, acquired history, and only for D_affordable/E the available allowance. They receive no actual k, realized full signature, omissions, parents, claim truth, hindsight subset, or unqueried actual answer. ## Exact reference and gap decomposition

[METHOD.md](METHOD.md) specifies the set-valued dynamic program, deterministic backpointers, and proofs. Retain every nondominated attainable pair (d,l), where d counts soundly detected signatures and l sums added realized costs over **all** compatible design signatures. Store full frontiers, canonical E policies, comparator points, and an attainable dominating witness whenever a comparator is dominated. Lower detection at lower cost is a tradeoff, not a matched-detection improvement. Randomized mixtures and other workload distributions are outside the exactness claim.

Independently enumerate the minimum extra contradictory subset for each root/signature. Nominal-compatible signatures have explicit `no_catalogue_contradiction`, not zero or an arbitrary penalty. Preserve minimum cost, tied-subset rule, typed observations, and checked contradiction certificate. These hindsight subsets never enter a selector.

At each root/budget retain: the hindsight-eligible count h(b), exact maximum dE(b), and each comparator's count. Decompose D_affordable's missed-signature **count** into (i) the count whose minimum certificate exceeds b, (ii) the search gap h(b)−dE(b), and (iii) the policy-selection gap dE(b)−dD_affordable(b). All terms are nonnegative when the exact solve is available. This arithmetic does not identify three disjoint named sets of misses: E and D_affordable can detect different signatures. The hindsight count is an upper bound, not an executable strategy or guarantee of simultaneous detection. Report zero gaps and comparator points already on the frontier without retuning.

Report shortest realized certificate cost, all-signature mean policy cost, cost on nominal-compatible signatures, and cost conditional on detection separately. Conditional detected subsets can differ. The all-signature objective must not erase expenditure on archives where no conflict exists.
