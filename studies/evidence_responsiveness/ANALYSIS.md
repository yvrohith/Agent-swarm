# Evidence responsiveness under a bounded evidence contract

This is a separate, review-informed exploratory follow-up. It asks whether an
investigator answers correctly on both sides of a decisive evidence change and
on both sides of a change that preserves the warranted answer. The completed
utility pilot, including ceiling, null, adverse and invalid outcomes, is retained.
Behavioral invariance/directional testing is established methodology; this is
neither a new identification theorem nor a test of internal reasoning faithfulness.

## Fixed design and selection

There are 12 evaluation families (eight receipt, four controlled wiki-derived)
and two separate development families (one of each substrate). Each has base,
irrelevant and decisive variants and one Boolean primary claim. The planned two
models therefore receive 72 evaluation and at most 12 development requests, each
in a fresh stateless context. Four additional transport retries are the maximum;
no content-based retries or repairs are allowed. Families, not requests, are the
measurement units. Development sources/instances are disjoint from evaluation.

Receipt evaluation has two independent instances, in opposite directions, of
each fixed motif: downstream context receipt presence, completeness recipient
scope, receipt recipient identity, and completeness time scope. Each contract
declares a target and two valid decoy scopes with fixed event slots. Source-copy
and independent shared-scaffold mechanisms can render the same target. Changes
to evidence never stand in for a changed hidden source-use label. Nuisance
changes use consistent opaque identifier renaming or timestamp-preserving
record reordering. The original finite oracle remains unchanged; a private
adapter restores its four query descriptions without changing any evidence or
assumption and selects the certified context-entry claim.

Wiki evaluation has two independent, opposite-direction instances each of
inserted-versus-inherited and exact-match character sensitivity. The pinned
official export is source material, not modified incident history. Eligibility
requires full bounded predecessor/target texts, one qualifying literal title
match suitable for an unambiguous checked transformation, the unchanged
navigation and matching rules, and at most 12,000 total text characters. Every
public evidence view is additionally bounded at 48,000 serialized UTF-8 bytes;
evidence is rejected rather than truncated. Prior extraction-review endpoints
and all utility-development/evaluation endpoints are excluded. Selection uses
the new fixed salt `tracebench-evidence-responsiveness-v1-20261003`; all five
chosen page-pair histories are disjoint. Rejections and selection ranks are
recorded before inference. The fixed motifs are selected strata, not a random
sample of forensic questions.

Transformations operate only on fixture copies. Every wiki variant carries the
same explicit controlled-fixture description: altered text is not a historical
observation. Presented full texts have their own hashes. Original source bytes,
publisher hashes, identities and exact edits stay in evaluator-side provenance.
The claim concerns insertion under Unicode, case-sensitive bounded-title
matching and `SequenceMatcher(autojunk=False)` character spans, not originality,
intent, exposure or source use. No census or old matching rule is changed.

## Certificates and information boundary

Every actual variant must independently reproduce its certificate. Receipt
certificates exhaust the declared nonempty finite possibility set and preserve
rendered constructions; unresolved status requires disagreeing valid worlds.
Wiki certificates record exact matches, hashes, opcodes and qualifying spans,
with a separate classification check using the same declared alignment rule.
No independent human validation is claimed. For each family, base and irrelevant
gold statuses must match and decisive gold must differ. Certification success
is a design/test property, not an investigator result.

One competent system/task prompt and output contract are fixed before development.
Only opaque case IDs, full records, material assumptions and one opaque claim
ID `q0` enter requests. Family IDs, roles, transformations, provenance, oracle
counts and gold remain evaluator-side. Mutating evaluator metadata must leave
requests identical. Complete development triplets, prompts and certificates
are inspected mechanically/with AI assistance before evaluation. Incident text
and URLs are inert; no tools, browsing, identity reconstruction or persistent
model conversations are permitted.

Answers must be JSON with exactly `case_id` and `answers`, containing one object
with `claim_id`, `status`, `evidence_ids` and a nonempty explanation of at most
400 characters. Status is `established`, `ruled_out` or `unresolved`. The old
strict answer parser is reused through a substrate-only adapter. Invalid,
missing, refused and truncated responses remain first responses and failures.
Provider reasoning is never requested as visible explanation or graded.

## Schedule, models and stopping

Families are ordered by salted SHA-256 using order seed 84103. Cycle the six
role permutations, offset by model, so each role occupies each position four
times across 12 evaluation families per model. Two development families use
the same deterministic scheme; exact three-way positional balance is impossible
with two families. Only opaque case requests reach the stateless endpoint.

Reuse the completed execution's requested aliases `openai/gpt-5.6-terra` and
`anthropic/claude-sonnet-5.5`, native provider routing, provider-default decoding
and reasoning, and 8,192 total output tokens including reasoning. Refresh actual
official source metadata and access before inference. Alias and returned model
identities are recorded without claiming immutable weights. A one-model fallback
is permitted only on pre-outcome access/cost grounds, retaining all 12 families.
Never choose a model by observed accuracy or add harder cases after an all-pass
result. No evaluation variant receives a development call.

The entire schedule is bounded before the first call. Cumulative authorization
is USD 25, including historical charges and unresolved reservations. The exact
historical ledger and all its costs remain preserved. The new fixed study ledger
has a distinct namespace and checks historical plus active new reservations
before every attempt. Missing cost metadata retains its full reservation. A
directory change, resume, or unknown refusal charge cannot reset spending. The
new configuration records the verified bound and ledger hashes; provider-reported
charges, conservative token estimates and unresolved reservations are separate.
Only `OPENROUTER_KEY` is read by this study's execution configuration.

After the two development families, freeze exact cases, mapping, certificates,
prompts, request order, models/settings, parser/scoring, code/dependency hashes,
denominators, examples, uncertainty rules and the remaining spending bound before
evaluation. Genuine technical corrections must be documented before this freeze;
completed outputs are never silently replaced or retrospectively reinterpreted.

## Outcomes and uncertainty

Report each model separately for receipt and controlled wiki-derived families:

1. Decisive-pair correctness: base and decisive both schema-valid and correct.
2. Invariant-pair correctness: base and irrelevant both schema-valid and correct.
3. Whole-family correctness: all three schema-valid and correct.

Changing an answer alone is not success; stable wrong answers are not correct
invariance. Missing members remain failures in fixed family denominators. Also
report each variant's accuracy, invalid/missing/refused counts, nonexistent-ID
errors, incorrect stability on decisive changes and incorrect changes on
irrelevant changes. Behavioral change/stability diagnostics are conditional on
two schema-valid answers and retain their eligible-pair counts separately.

Companion outcomes require each relevant answer to be status-correct and cite
at least one valid supplied record/assumption ID with no nonexistent IDs. This
mechanical support check is not semantic entailment or explanation faithfulness.
Empty citations can pass status correctness but cannot pass this companion.
No LLM grader or semantic repair is introduced. Constant-status baselines require
zero calls; always-unresolved must have zero decisive-pair and whole-family
correctness because every decisive pair changes gold.

Use 2,000 percentile bootstrap resamples with seed 84104, resampling whole
family/history clusters while keeping triplets together. Keep shared histories
in one cluster. Report family/cluster counts; do not pool models or substrates.
Intervals describe these small selected strata. All-pass/all-fail zero-width
intervals do not imply universal performance or zero generalization uncertainty.

For each model/substrate, select the first failed decisive pair and the first
failed invariant pair by the SHA-256 ordering of the base case ID (family ID
breaks ties). If none qualify, explicitly report no observed failure of that
type. Do not select dramatic examples after inspecting answers.

## Artifacts and interpretation

Save raw exact requests/responses, full fixture texts and transformations in
ignored `artifacts/evidence-responsiveness/`. The curated study contains protocol,
configuration, preservation hashes, public/request hash manifests, evaluator
mapping/certificate metadata, execution accounting, scores and a report/table.
The preservation snapshot retains all 138 original tracked-file hashes and 415
historical raw-file hashes. Exactly `README.md` and `docs/SUBMISSION.md` are
presentation-only exceptions for actual-result links after reporting; all 136
other tracked files and all historical raw files must remain byte-identical.
Checks list those presentation changes explicitly. Earlier manifests/checkers
remain untouched, including their historical root-document pins.

An authorized local scorer can reproduce results from retained exact responses;
public hashes alone are not access to those contents. No raw response or incident
material is published automatically.

Controlled wiki-derived fixtures are not new incident observations. An all-pass
outcome applies only to these certified transformations under the supplied
contract; a failure identifies behavior here, not an internal causal mechanism.
This does not revise the utility pilot's limited result. Stop after this fixed
study; no case expansion, assistance arms, model shopping or UI work. This task
does not authorize committing, pushing, deployment or submission.
