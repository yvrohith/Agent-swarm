# Evidence-responsiveness follow-up

**Terra answered both sides of every decisive pair correctly, but changed to a wrong answer after one irrelevant wiki edit. Sonnet’s paired scores were dominated by unusable responses.** This separate, review-informed exploratory study preserves the completed utility pilot’s ceiling, null, adverse, and response-contract findings.

The question is whether an investigator responds correctly to a decisive evidence change while remaining correct after an irrelevant change. The frozen design has eight receipt and four controlled wiki-derived evaluation families, plus two separate development families. Every family has three certified variants and one claim. Each variant receives a fresh, stateless call under one fixed investigation prompt. Investigators see the full supplied evidence and assumptions; family linkage, roles, certificates and gold stay private to evaluation.

The evaluation completed **72 calls**, after **12 development calls**, with **zero retries**. There are 12 evaluation families, not 72 independent problems. The table gives successes/families and percentile 95% intervals for rates, resampling whole family/history clusters 2,000 times. Models and substrates are separate.

A decisive-pair success requires correct answers on both sides, and an invariant-pair success requires both answers to remain correct. A flip alone is insufficient. The companion requiring nonempty valid evidence IDs gives identical paired results; identifier existence does not establish semantic support or faithful reasoning. Always-unresolved has zero decisive-pair and whole-family success by construction, a scoring-control property rather than an investigator discovery.

Terra returned 36 valid evaluation answers, of which 35 were correct. Its single incorrect answer is a genuine status error under the certified fixture contract: the base and irrelevant variants both warrant `established`, but the irrelevant variant received `ruled_out`. This is a behavioral inconsistency on one controlled comparison, not evidence of its internal cause. Its receipt all-pass outcome and absence of failed decisive pairs are retained without harder replacement cases.

Sonnet returned 10 valid, correct answers and 26 schema-invalid responses, including two empty refusals. Invalid/refused outputs remain failures with the full denominators. These results concern response-contract reliability; they do not establish that all failed pairs reflected an incorrect evidence interpretation. No invalid response was repaired or resubmitted. Cases, prompt, aliases and scoring were unchanged after development outcomes.

Requested and returned aliases matched: `openai/gpt-5.6-terra` through OpenAI and `anthropic/claude-sonnet-5.5` through Anthropic, with 42 responses each including development. These are aliases, not immutable weight revisions. The same native-provider routing, default decoding/reasoning, and 8,192 total output-token limit from the prior execution were retained.

Known new provider-reported charges total **USD 0.5853300**. Two new responses lack authoritative charges and retain **USD 0.198520** in reservations. Including the prior study, known charges total **USD 3.4468440**, unresolved reservations **USD 0.335912**, and charged-or-reserved accounting **USD 3.7827560**, below the cumulative USD 25 ceiling. Actual total cost remains unknown; provider metadata are not an invoice.

**Limits:** small, selected motif strata; one realization per variant/model; mechanical and AI-assisted certification without independent human validation; shared alignment implementation in the wiki checks; possible prior familiarity with public text; aliases and provider defaults can change. All-pass/all-fail bootstrap intervals do not imply universal performance. Receipt claims concern exposure under an explicit finite contract. Controlled wiki-derived texts are artificial comparisons, never newly discovered historical observations. The study does not measure source-use attribution on the real incident or revise the earlier utility comparison.

All 42 variants were independently recertified before evaluation. Validation passed **620 tests (189 new)** and Ruff. Historical scientific files and responses remain byte-identical. Only the two project entry points receive new result links after reporting; the [preservation policy](preservation.json) retains their original hashes and records the narrow presentation exception.
