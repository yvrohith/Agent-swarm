# Finite retained-evidence acquisition: results

**Pair-cut saves retrieval cost on average in this fixed model, but does not dominate the schema-aware baseline or attain the exact optimum.** It loses to schema-aware ordering in the positive-receipt stratum and on seven individual problems. The finite archive, shared checker and assumptions determine which claims can eventually be resolved; no policy creates missing evidence.

## Execution and units

The [freeze](freeze.json) was written at `2026-10-04T00:31:47.990033+00:00` after development and 751 passing offline tests, before evaluation-policy outcomes. Four separate development problems produced 950 signature/policy/budget runs and zero certificate failures.

The completed evaluation uses **40 problems, five policies and five budgets**: 1,000 problem/policy/budget rows, 9,800 exact signature/policy/budget runs, 19,300 charged passive lookups, and 1,922 unique certificates. Across problems there are 1,064 hypothetical assignments, 392 full-signature cells and 224 catalogue actions. Worlds/signatures/repeated budgets are not independent scientific problems. There are **30 distinct unweighted structures**, with ten price variants of repeated structures; all 40 cost-sensitive fingerprints differ. This is a complete fixed finite-model calculation, not a random incident sample.

Costs below are abstract retrieval units. Expectations sum exact rational prior masses within each problem, then equally average problems within the stated group. Canonical bytes count observable lookup payloads, including nonzero empty-lookup messages. No confidence intervals or significance claims are attached to this complete bounded calculation.

## Reproduction, preservation and limits

From the existing installed environment, `.venv/bin/python -m tracebench.evidence_acquisition verify` checks the complete frozen closure. The saved evaluation can be recomputed only into a fresh ignored directory with `.venv/bin/python -m tracebench.evidence_acquisition run --output artifacts/evidence-acquisition/replication`; it refuses existing outputs. This report uses the retained first evaluation and does not authorize another model call. Preparation/development/freezing commands likewise refuse to overwrite saved inputs.

[Per-problem/policy/budget data](results/per_problem_policy_budget.json), [stratified summaries and paired contrasts](results/summary.json), [runtime accounting](results/runtime.json), and [output hashes](results/manifest.json) retain the numerical results. [Certificates](results/certificates.json.gz) and [signature-run index](results/signature_runs.json.gz) are compact lossless gzip JSON with deliberately synthetic content. Signature indices address lexicographically sorted full signatures in the corresponding frozen problem. Certificates include acquired histories and can be independently reconstructed and checked against [the public synthetic inputs](evaluation_problems.json). Expand larger histories only under ignored `artifacts/evidence-acquisition/`.

[Independent verification](results/independent_verification.json) and [validation/preservation](validation.json) record the completed checks separately from the frozen protocol/results. No scientific source, prior scores, raw response, refusal, cost ledger or historical freeze was replaced. The [review-remediation record](../review_remediation/REVIEW.md) explains the isolated legacy chronology defect and current score verifier.

Finite enumeration, certain-answer semantics, indistinguishability, dynamic programming and equivalence-class-style acquisition are established ideas. This study tests their application under incomplete retention, not a new general theorem or optimizer. Results depend on the closed catalogue, authentic receipts, scoped completeness, explicit source-use/shared-input alternatives, supplied priors and costs. Thirty unweighted structures do not establish deployment generality. Irreducibility applies only to the permitted archive; a different external record could change the evidence boundary. No cases, heuristics, prices, priors or tie rules were changed after evaluation to improve these comparisons.
