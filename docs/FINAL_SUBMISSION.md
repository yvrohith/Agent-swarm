# Trace Completeness Curves

**Code:** <https://github.com/yvrohith/Agent-swarm> · [Reviewer guide](REVIEWER_GUIDE.md)

## Abstract

What can incomplete agent records support, and where do investigators fail to deliver a usable, justified answer? Trace Completeness Curves connects a structural simulation, an observation-only logging-loss experiment, a public-wiki reference audit, and two bounded model-investigator evaluations. It separates available evidence, actual source selection, response validity, and warranted conclusions. Complete synthetic logs improve source-edge precision without guaranteeing a better aggregate estimate. Retaining exposure evidence under logging loss helps one heuristic's target classification while harming another's. In the wiki export, most eligible snapshot reference pairs contain inherited text, which cannot establish copying. The utility pilot does not demonstrate a general reasoning benefit from provenance assistance. The responsiveness evaluation exposes two different failures: rejected serialization and one valid, incorrect conclusion within a controlled invariant pair. Frozen artifacts and an offline diagnosis make these distinctions inspectable. The contribution is an auditable measurement tool and bounded findings, not verified transmission in a historical incident.

## Evidence availability and attribution

The [synthetic benchmark](../results/REPORT.md) measures direct realized source selection: the simulator selected a particular earlier output from another run. This is neither counterfactual necessity nor intent. Temporal proximity proposes nearby same-page predecessors; witness reuse proposes predecessors sharing an exact token. Five observations of each fixed world reveal writes, stable identities, requests, delivery, then context entry. Exposure can remain unused, and shared scaffolds can produce matching text.

Complete authentic logging preserves true candidates by design. Constant recall and nondecreasing defined precision are structural properties, not discoveries. At transmission parameter 0.3 and shock 0.9, measured witness precision rises from 8.2% to 75.3%, while recall stays 52.1%. Yet absolute target-fraction error rises from 0.030 with requests to 0.059 with context. A small signed aggregate error can hide cancellation between falsely attributed and missed targets. Edge errors therefore remain separate from target errors.

The [missing-receipt follow-up](../studies/missing_receipts/results/REPORT.md) removes observable receipts after world generation, preserving events, truth and every eligible write. An unavailable telemetry type differs from missing records within a type; neither proves non-occurrence. The comparison policy accepts trusted context-entry evidence despite missing upstream receipts and reports unknown exposure when completeness cannot justify exclusion. Authentication is assumed, not cryptographically implemented. Supported exposure still produces only heuristic source candidates.

With 49.97% actual delivery-record retention, positive-use parameter 0.3 and shock 0.9, evidence-aware minus conjunction target disagreement is **−3.31 percentage points** for witness reuse, with a 95% interval of **[−3.68, −2.93]**, but **+14.99 [13.90, 16.07]** for temporal proximity. These [paired contrasts](../studies/missing_receipts/results/study.json) bootstrap 20 worlds, not edges or masks. Preserved evidence improves recall without uniformly improving attribution.

The [public-wiki audit](../studies/wiki_case_study/REPORT.md) asks a different question: which exact page-name references overlap newly inserted character spans? Its [saved counts](../studies/wiki_case_study/results/counts.json) distinguish 12,536 source-order-eligible snapshot pairs from 10,520 attribution-eligible pairs. The latter divide into 3,140 inserted and 7,380 inherited-only pairs, or 70.15% inherited-only. The 2,016-pair eligibility reduction is separate from inherited text. Inserted pairs contain 4,854 occurrences across 2,500 edits; occurrences and pairs are different units. Another 2,519 revisions are excluded or ambiguous, with overlapping reasons.

These counts depend on a fixed character-alignment rule. Generic titles and provider names can match without establishing an intentional reference. The public export lacks stable run identities and authenticated read/delivery/context records; exposure remains unknown and source use unobserved. This measures attribution-rule sensitivity, not a copying rate or source-use false-positive rate. Comparing revision deltas addresses an existing cumulative-text problem documented by Lütje, not a newly discovered phenomenon.

## Usable answers and warranted conclusions

The [utility pilot](../studies/investigator_utility/openrouter_v1/results/REPORT.md) scores what supplied evidence warrants. Its primary comparison adds deterministic provenance assistance to raw evidence plus a strong checklist (C versus B); raw evidence alone is secondary A. Assistance supplies indexing and alignments, without privileged labels. Warranted-answer accuracy rewards valid correct definite answers; unjustified certainty counts valid definite answers where the evidence is unresolved. Invalid answers cannot earn successful-restraint credit. Both model studies use aliases `openai/gpt-5.6-terra` and `anthropic/claude-sonnet-5.5`.

Terra's accuracy was 100% in every arm and substrate. Sonnet's C−B warranted-answer differences were +18.75 points [0, 37.50] on 16 wiki cases and +28.57 [−28.57, 71.43] on seven applicable synthetic cases. All primary intervals include zero. Its wiki C−A difference was zero [−18.75, 18.75]. Every schema-valid status was correct, so observed differences concern response acceptance. C also invalidated a previously valid synthetic response and introduced a nonexistent wiki citation. Unjustified certainty was zero throughout, using 16 wiki and five applicable synthetic cases. The synthetic certainty-or-invalid diagnostic remained 40% in B and C. Explanations were not semantically graded; no general reasoning benefit is established.

The separate [responsiveness study](../studies/evidence_responsiveness/results/REPORT.md) uses one prompt and fresh calls for base, irrelevant-change and decisive-change variants. Certification precedes evaluation. Success requires both sides of a pair, or all three family members, to be correct. There are **12 evaluation families, not 72 independent problems**. Terra's controlled-wiki invariant success is 3/4, with family-bootstrap interval [0.25, 1.00]; its receipt families all pass. Sonnet's 26 rejected responses and ten accepted/correct responses do not imply 26 incorrect evidence judgments. Strict evidence-ID results match the primary scores, but ID existence does not establish semantic support. Constant-status controls are design checks.

The [offline diagnosis](../studies/evidence_responsiveness/offline_failure_audit/REPORT.md), not a sixth experiment, partitions Sonnet's 36 responses into 12 with prose plus one JSON object, eight with two objects and self-correction prose, two refusals, two using `id` instead of `claim_id`, two with an extra `id`, and ten accepted/correct. All 42 identifiable objects use arrays: ambiguous “answers must contain one object” wording explains **zero observed array-shape failures**. Twenty nonempty outputs acquire cascading `missing_response` flags; only two lack final content. No result-changing adapter/scorer defect was found. Embedded answers were never substituted for rejected completions.

Terra's audited family `f_9695d2661a05a12eced27723` instead contains a valid wrong answer. Certified statuses are established / established / ruled_out; answers are established / ruled_out / ruled_out. The irrelevant change at `r1[898]`, outside title span `[877,896)`, preserves inserted-reference status. The decisive predecessor change at `r0[886]` makes the reference inherited. Exact [request differences](../studies/evidence_responsiveness/offline_failure_audit/terra_summary.json) include opaque case IDs and derived hashes too. This verifies one controlled behavioral inconsistency, not a causally isolated edit effect or historical conclusion. Both certificate checks share SequenceMatcher alignment; neither is human validation.

## Inspection and practical limits

Small selected cases, one sampled completion per variant, changeable model aliases, shared alignment machinery and possible public-text familiarity limit generalization. Whole worlds or case/history families, rather than repeated calls, underpin intervals; all-pass intervals do not imply universal reliability. These studies do not establish a new identification theorem or an optimal logging policy.

[Accounting](../studies/evidence_responsiveness/results/execution.json) remains $3.446844 known charges plus $0.335912 reservations, totaling $3.782756 charged-or-reserved; exact actual cost is unknown. The prior audit reported 638 passing tests and 771 preserved input hashes. Those checks support implementation integrity, not scientific novelty. The [reviewer guide](REVIEWER_GUIDE.md) maps claims to their saved sources and explains reproduction and access limits.

Tracked tables permit claim inspection and reaggregation. Exact fixtures and final answers remain in an ignored local package; hashes alone cannot enable independent response rescoring. Publication permissions, team attribution and final submission remain user-owned. [Organizer requirements](HACKATHON.md) and [source status](RELATED_WORK.md) are historical records, not newly verified requirements. Preparation is not publication or submission.

The supported practical lesson is to distinguish missing evidence from negative evidence, inspect inherited text before attributing additions, and report response usability alongside warranted conclusions. Better exposure records do not by themselves establish source use, and valid serialization does not guarantee a correct answer.
