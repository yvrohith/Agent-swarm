# Limitations

1. **Synthetic assumptions determine the result.** Shared tasks, launch waves, witness generation, access, delivery, context insertion, and source selection are generated by a small explicit model. These mechanisms are useful controls, not a validated model of real LLM behavior. Performance must be reported with the exact configuration.

2. **The label records use, not necessity.** The evaluator knows which prior output was used during generation. It does not measure a real agent's mental process or the causal effect of withholding information. A used source need not be necessary for an output that could also have been produced independently.

3. **Context membership is incomplete evidence.** An investigator may see a source in context without knowing whether it was selected. Context telemetry covers shared-channel chunks, not every latent task or scaffold input. Keeping the selected source hidden avoids converting the richest observation regime into an oracle. Successful delivery alone is an even weaker signal. Responses are inserted fully or dropped; the model does not simulate partial token truncation.

4. **Estimator performance is not an identification theorem.** Better scores show a benefit for a particular investigator in a particular model. Failure by these simple investigators does not prove that every estimator must fail, and perfect performance on a finite sweep does not prove identifiability.

5. **The timing baseline is deliberately simple.** Temporal proximity can be confounded by launch waves or shared tasks. It is not a fitted Hawkes process; its errors cannot be presented as errors of every temporal or point-process method.

6. **Witnesses rely on generation assumptions.** Synthetic witness generation and copying are controlled. False witnesses are modeled through independently emitted shared-scaffold strings, not a calibrated accidental hash-collision process. Real strings may come from unseen sources, prompts, templates, or memory. Missed copying and paraphrases can lower recall; apparent novelty need not establish provenance.

7. **Clean telemetry imposes several metric directions.** Every true candidate has compatible request, delivery, and context records, so filtering preserves true-positive counts and recall. Candidate counts and false-positive counts cannot increase; precision, where defined at both levels for the same world, cannot decrease. These properties are structural, not independent discoveries. Baselines can miss true edges initially because of the temporal window or omitted witnesses. The estimated target fraction decreases or stays constant, while its absolute error can increase or decrease.

8. **Stable identity and reliable logs are idealizations.** A known run mapping is more reliable than reconstructed identities in most incidents. Revealed logs are complete and noiseless in the original baseline. The separate [missing-receipt follow-up](studies/missing_receipts/results/REPORT.md) tests nested erasure of authentic delivery or context records at fixed worlds. It assumes correct coarse completeness declarations and trusted receipts; it does not implement cryptographic authentication. Forged, reordered, run-correlated, adversarially selected, or jointly missing logs require further stress tests. Neither this limited loss model nor the clean telemetry ladder validates a real logging system.

9. **Repeated seeds do not establish external validity.** They measure variability within this generator and parameter sweep. They are not independent incidents, uncertainty about the real world, or evidence that a setting is representative.

10. **The public-wiki audit is descriptive, not source-use validation.** Its pinned export supports revision text and limited event metadata, but lacks stable run identities and authenticated source-read/delivery/context evidence. Character diffs and conservative timestamp intervals cannot recover missing history, establish original authorship, or rule out alternative sources. Supplemental sites supply recovered added lines rather than full histories. Observed reference counts are not copying rates, precision, recall or bounds on synthetic source use. See the separate [case-study report](studies/wiki_case_study/results/REPORT.md).

11. **Exact submission-form constraints remain unverified.** The organizer's home and logistics pages were checked on October 3, 2026; they specify the core deliverables and an October 4 deadline at 8 p.m. Eastern / 5 p.m. Pacific. No detailed scoring rubric appears on those pages. The linked submission form returned HTTP 403, leaving its exact field limits and terms unverified. See `docs/HACKATHON.md` for provenance and remaining checks.

The project is a reproducible measurement experiment. It does not determine malicious intent, detect every form of coordination, establish a bound by combining unrelated temporal and witness statistics, or substitute for source verification in an incident investigation.
