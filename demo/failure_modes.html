The fixed literal-attribution rule: match the exact case-sensitive title at Unicode word boundaries. Compare full predecessor and target texts with SequenceMatcher(autojunk=False). A match is inserted if a title character overlaps an insertion or replacement. It is inherited only when an equal alignment maps the whole match to the same bounded title in the predecessor. A boundary created solely by deletion is neither category. No target match makes the insertion claim false.

  1. Base: the target title Jan14PovertyWatcher occupies [877,896). The predecessor excerpt is Jan14PoveQtyWatcher. Replacement [886,887) overlaps the title, so the target occurrence is inserted.
  2. Irrelevant change: r1[898]: M → Q is outside the title, after two newlines. The original replacement at [886,887) and title match at [877,896) remain. The insertion claim stays established.
  3. Decisive change: r0[886]: Q → r restores the predecessor title. The match now lies inside equal span [0,2557), so it is inherited. The later insertion [2557,2765) has no other matching title. The insertion claim becomes ruled_out.

Offsets are zero-based Unicode code points; half-open spans include the start and exclude the end. “Inserted” is an alignment category, not authorship, exposure, copying, or source use.

One completion per variant; case IDs and derived hashes change too. This verifies one controlled behavioral inconsistency, not a causally isolated effect of the character edit. Both certificate checks share SequenceMatcher; neither is human validation. Valid evidence IDs do not establish semantic support.

Interpretation

Usability and evidence correctness need separate checks

The responsiveness study contains 12 evaluation families, not 72 independent problems. These two examples diagnose specific observed failures; they do not rank general model ability. The completed utility pilot also does not establish a general reasoning benefit from provenance assistance: Terra was at ceiling, and Sonnet’s observed differences were driven by response acceptance.

All results here come from saved artifacts. This static demonstration adds no model calls, experiments or response repairs. The existing explorer covers the initial synthetic benchmark; the final write-up connects the completed studies and their distinct evidence limits.
